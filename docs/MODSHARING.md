# VirtualTSZ Precision Farming Extension

**Your soil no longer lasts forever.** Precision Farming tracks nitrogen and pH, but not the phosphorus, potassium and humus that leave the field with every harvest. This mod adds that missing part.

## New layers

Three new Precision Farming soil maps, revealed by soil sampling and shown in the map menu and the field info box:

- **P2O5** (phosphorus)
- **K2O** (potassium)
- **OM** (organic matter / humus)

The maps show the distance from the target of the soil type: 0 is on target, minus is a deficit, plus is a surplus.

## New products

11 fertilizers in the shop (solids in big bags, liquids in IBCs):

- Nitrogen: CAN 27, Urea 46, Nitrosol UAN 30 (liquid)
- Phosphorus: MAP 11-52-0, Triple superphosphate 0-46-0, Single superphosphate 0-18-0
- Potassium: Potassium chloride 60 MOP, Patentkali 0-0-30
- Mixed: NPK 15-15-15, NPK 8-24-24
- Yeast vinasse (liquid): cheap potassium, a little N and organic matter

Manure, chopped straw, legumes, alfalfa and catch crops also feed the maps.

## Key features

- Soil runs down when harvests take out more than you put back. Silage, potatoes and sugar beet take the most.
- Yield only drops when a nutrient is short. The mod never adds extra yield.
- Automatic P and K application rate from the revealed map, plus manual rate keys.
- Application HUD and minimap layer.
- Works with fertilizing sowing machines.
- In-game help page with the nutrient needs of every crop.
- Five worked example crop rotations.
- Real agronomy data (VDLUFA humus balance, LfL Bavaria, Hungarian soil data), simplified for the game.
- Multiplayer: install on the server and on every client.

## Dependencies

- **Precision Farming DLC** (`FS25_precisionFarming`)
- **[TR] Precision Farming Configurator** (`FS25_0_TRPFConfigurator`) by ThundR. Download it here (free Patreon subscription to ThundRFS is enough): https://www.patreon.com/ThundRFS/posts/precision-v1-2-0-141726084?collection=1845723

Made for the Szalók map; other maps should work in principle but are untested.
